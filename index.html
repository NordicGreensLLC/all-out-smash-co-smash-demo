RAGE ROOMS

Step into one of our four fully equipped rage rooms and let it all out. Grab a sledgehammer, baseball bat, or crowbar and demolish everything from old office printers and computer monitors to dishes, glassware, and even a framed photo of your ex. Our rooms are designed for maximum destruction in a completely safe, controlled environment. Every session includes full safety gear—coveralls, gloves, face shield, and closed-toe shoe covers—so you can swing with total confidence. Rage rooms are the ultimate stress reliever, turning your worst day into your best story. Whether you are dealing with work pressure, relationship frustration, or simply craving an adrenaline rush, smashing things to pieces delivers a cathartic release unlike anything else in Denver.

SPLATTER ROOMS

unleash your inner artist - no talent required. Our two dedicated splatter rooms transform you into a paint-flinging rebel. AXE THROWING

Channel your inner lumberjack in our four dedicated axe-throwing tunnels - located right next to the Smash Shack bar, so a cold craft beer or signature cocktail is never more than a few steps away. Our experienced staff will coach you through proper grip, stance, and release technique, so even first-timers are sticking bullseyes within minutes. Axe throwing combines focus, precision, and a healthy dose of competitive spirit, making it one of the most talked-about activities in Denver. Formerly known as Axe Whooping, our axe-throwing program has moved under the All Out Smash roof, bringing together the best of both worlds.

Experience the Ultimate Adrenaline Rush at All Out Smash in Denver

4 Rage Rooms, 2 Splatter Rooms, and 4 Axe-Throwing Tunnels

Are you looking for an adrenaline-pumping adventure or just a fun way to blow off steam? At All Out Smash, we’ve got you covered. Below, we’ll break down each of our thrilling experiences from Rage Rooms and Splatter Rooms to Axe-Throwing Tunnels, so you can find the perfect way to let loose and have a smashing good time!

4 Rage Rooms

We offer four fully equipped Rage Rooms designed to help you let loose and relieve stress in a safe, controlled environment. Each session is booked in 30-minute increments, giving you plenty of time to smash, bash, and break away from your everyday worries. Our scheduling ensures there’s ample availability throughout the day; simply reserve a time slot that fits your schedule and get ready to unleash your inner demolition expert!

2 Splatter Rooms

Get ready to channel your inner artist (or paint-throwing rebel) in our two dedicated Splatter Rooms. With two tunnels available throughout the day, reserving your spot is as easy as picking the perfect time for your next adrenaline rush!

Questions
and answers

1Is There an Age Limit?
The age limits vary by activity: Rage Room & Axe-Throwing • Minimum age: 10 years. • Anyone under 18 must arrive with a parent or legal guardian who will sign the waiver and stay in the room/lane during the session. • We provide youth-sized safety gear, but please be sure younger guests can comfortably handle the tools before booking. Splatter Room • All ages welcome! Little artists, date-night duos, and grandparents alike can sling paint together. Kids 12 and under should still have an adult nearby to help with goggles and paint ponchos. If you have questions about a specific child’s readiness, especially for rage smashing or axe throwing, give us a call. Our staff is happy to walk you through safety requirements and help you choose the best All Out Smash experience for your crew.
2What Should You Wear?
To ensure the safety of our customers, all participants must wear long pants, socks that cover their ankles, and closed-toed, flat shoes only. NO shorts, dresses, skirts, sandals, flip-flops, high heels, or open-toed shoes of any kind will be permitted. Please keep in mind smashing is much like a workout, and you will break a sweat, so we suggest lightweight, breathable clothing, such as workout attire, if possible. Long sleeves and rugged, thick-soled shoes are required.
3What Times Can You Schedule?
Hours of operation: Tuesday-Sunday - 12pm to 8pm Monday - Closed 4 smash rooms: Appointments will be booked in 30-minute increments. We will have availability scheduled for 4 available smash rooms.
4Can You Donate Your Unwanted Items?
We care about our community and about the environment, so being socially responsible and eco-friendly is one of our top priorities. All Out Smash is happy to accept all of your electronics, un-upholstered furniture, glass, cups, mugs, windows, doors, ceramics, household items, and pretty much anything smashable. Smashing these materials into pieces and proper disposal significantly reduces their decomposition time, which is ultimately better for the environment. The only things we cannot take are tube TVs, vacuum cleaners, BBQ grills, upholstered furniture, mattresses, and light bulbs of any kind.
5Refund & Rescheduling Policy?
All Out Smash has a no refund policy. Once you make your appointment, we go to work purchasing items for your experience. You are able to freely reschedule your break room experience up to 24 hours before you are scheduled to enter the break room.
